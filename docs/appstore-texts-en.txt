--- DESCRIPTION (max. 4000 characters) ---

Hippominder reliably reminds you of all important appointments for your horse – farrier, vaccinations, and deworming.

Never forget an appointment again! With the unique flower display, you can see at a glance when each appointment is due. The closer a deadline gets, the more clearly the flower shows it – from fresh green to warning red.

FEATURES:

- Appointment management for farrier, vaccinations, and deworming
- Individual intervals for each horse
- Visual flower display with color gradient
- Color-coded traffic light indicators (green/orange/red)
- Email notifications to stable owner, farrier & more
- Contact management with Apple Contacts integration
- Horse photos from your photo library
- Calendar integration
- Local reminders
- 3 selectable color schemes
- Completely offline – no cloud, no tracking

GET STARTED FOR FREE:

Manage 1 horse completely free. With the one-time purchase of "Hippominder Pro", you unlock unlimited horses – no subscription, buy once, use forever.

PRIVACY:

All data stays on your device. Hippominder uses no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

Developed with love for all horse people.
