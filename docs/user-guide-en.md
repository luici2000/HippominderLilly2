# Hippominder – User Guide

## Why Hippominder?

As a horse owner, you know the struggle: When was the farrier last here? When is the next vaccination due? Is the deworming overdue?

**The Problem:** Horses need regular care at fixed intervals – hoof care every 6-8 weeks, vaccinations every 6-12 months, deworming every 3 months. Forgetting appointments means health risks for your horse.

**The Solution:** Hippominder shows you at a glance what's due and when. The unique flower display makes it visually clear – from green (all good) to orange (due soon) to red (overdue). No calendar chaos, no forgotten notes.

**Your Benefits:**
- Never forget an appointment again
- All horses at a glance
- Automatic reminders
- Email templates for farrier, vet & more
- Completely offline – no cloud, no tracking, your data stays with you

## Getting Started

### 1. Add a Horse
Tap the **"Pferd +"** icon in the top right. Enter your horse's name and optionally choose a photo from your library. The photo is automatically cropped to a circle.

### 2. Set Intervals
For each horse, you can set individual intervals:
- **Farrier:** Default 42 days (6 weeks). Use the +/- buttons or tap the number for direct input.
- **Vaccination:** Default 180 days (6 months)
- **Deworming:** Default 90 days (3 months)

### 3. Enter Last Appointments
Enter when the last appointment took place. Hippominder automatically calculates when the next one is due.

## Main View

### Horse List
Each horse shows:
- **Photo** (or placeholder silhouette)
- **Name**
- **Next due appointment** with day count and color indicator
- **3 status dots** (Farrier, Vaccination, Deworming) – green/orange/red

### Color Scheme
In the top right, you'll find 3 color spheres. Choose your favorite color scheme – it changes the flower display color for all horses.

### Contacts
Tap **"Kontakte"** in the top left to manage your important contacts: Vet, Farrier, Stable Owner, Pharmacist, Owner.

---

## Detail View

Tap a horse to open the detail view.

### Flower Timer
The large flower in the center shows the appointment status visually:
- **Green petals** = Plenty of time
- **Orange/yellow petals** = Due soon
- **Red petals** = Overdue or almost due

Switch between the 3 appointment types (Farrier, Vaccination, Deworming) by tapping the corresponding buttons.

### Update Appointment
Below the flower you'll find:
- **Last appointment:** Tap the date to change it
- **Next appointment:** Calculated automatically
- **Interval:** Adjust days with +/- or tap the number

### Confirm Appointment
When an appointment has taken place, tap **"Confirm Appointment"** – the date is set to today and the timer restarts.

### Send Email
Tap the mail icon to send a prepared email to the responsible contact (e.g., appointment request to the farrier).

## Notifications

Hippominder can remind you via push notification:
- Set how many days before the due date you want to be reminded (default: 3 days)
- Choose who should additionally be notified by email
